Public Steam presentation data:
Optional small Steam game-list icons and achievement icons are read from the
local Steam image cache or requested from Valve's public Steam CDN and cached
on the user's device. The game-list icon hash is read from client metadata;
public app hubs provide a fallback when that metadata is absent. Store covers
are not used for game-list thumbnails. These images are not bundled in the
executable. Game artwork, achievement names and associated trademarks remain
the property of their respective owners. Documentation screenshots show actual
widgets with public artwork and synthetic account/states, not a user's account.
Global completion percentages come from the public Steam Web API; image
filenames come from Steam Community's public global-achievement pages.

Steam achievement-set data:
Set definitions and reported account completion are read from Steam Community's
server-rendered achievement pages. No cookies or credentials are used. Steam
controls completion awards and persistence. No Valve JavaScript is copied or
executed by the application; the embedded JSON is parsed as data.

Public App ID catalog:
The bundled assets/catalog.json contains factual numeric Steam App IDs derived
from https://gib.me/sam/games.xml, the public game list used by SAM. Source and
retrieval time are embedded in the catalog. Names, ownership and personal library
information are not included in this catalog. Updates contact that third-party
host only when the user chooses Update catalog; no account information is sent.
