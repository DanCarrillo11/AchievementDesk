# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and specific. Critique ideas and behavior without
insults, harassment, threats or discriminatory language. Respect other people's
privacy and do not publish their account details, credentials or private messages.

Keep issues and pull requests relevant to Achievement Desk. Explain the problem,
provide reproducible evidence when possible and allow others time to respond.

For a sensitive conduct concern, request a private contact method without naming
people or sharing sensitive details in public. Repository maintainers may remove
abusive content, close disruptive discussions or restrict participation to keep
the project usable. GitHub's platform rules also apply.
